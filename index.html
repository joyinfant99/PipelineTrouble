HOW TO PLAY

1 Move with your keys and shoot straight up to pop the blockers floating above you.
2 Big blockers split into two smaller ones when popped. Every pop scores — smaller pieces are worth more.
3 Clearing a whole deal banks a big bonus. Grab floating pickups for points, a life, rapid fire, or a shield.
4 Don't let a blocker touch you — it costs one of your lives and knocks points off your score.
5 Hit the target score before the clock runs out to win the quarter.
