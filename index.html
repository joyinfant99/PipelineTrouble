HOW TO PLAY

1 Move with your keys and shoot straight up to pop the blockers floating above you.
2 Big blockers split into two smaller ones when popped. Keep shooting until nothing's left.
3 Fully clearing a deal adds MRR to your pipeline. Grab floating bonus bubbles for extra MRR, an extra life, rapid fire, or a shield.
4 Don't let a blocker touch you — it costs a life and knocks MRR off your pipeline.
5 Reach €100,000 MRR before the clock or your lives run out to win the quarter.
